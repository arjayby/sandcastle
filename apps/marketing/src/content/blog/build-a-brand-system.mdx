A logo can make a strong first impression. A Brand System makes that impression consistent wherever your company appears.

## Give every choice a job

A complete system gives every choice a job. It connects your logo, color, typography, voice, photography, motion, interface foundation, and Design Tokens. The connection makes each Brand Artifact feel like part of the same company.

<Callout title="Start with the whole system">
  A logo is one Brand Artifact. Define the rules around it before you ask it to carry your complete identity.
</Callout>

## Review the connections

Look at the system as one composition. A strong color choice can still fail if the typography and voice tell a different story.

<InteractiveExample summary="Check system coherence">
  Compare one message across your website, product interface, and social image. The details can adapt, but the intent and personality must stay recognizable.
</InteractiveExample>

## Revise with intent

Use a Semantic Revision when one part feels wrong. State the brand outcome that you need. The Brand Agent can then update the target and the directly dependent Brand Regions together.
